Atharv Umap Learning and Development Intern

A year of learning about myself, the people around me, and what it means to lead in service of a community.

Begin ↓

01 · Journey

How I got here.

  1. Spring 2025

    Participant

    I was first introduced to the MLEAD community by participating in the Organizational Leadership track.

  2. Fall 2025

    Facilitator

    I returned to the program as a Facilitator for the Foundational Leadership track.

  3. Spring 2026

    Intern

    I took my knowledge as a facilitator and a leadership minor to apply it to the Learning and Development Intern role.

Why this role and what did you hope to learn from this internship?

What I hoped for most was to grow in my ability to explain complex ideas clearly, while continuing to practice coaching and inspiring the people I worked with. Inclusivity was a constant effort. Every cohort brought new people with different experiences and perspectives, so I wanted to keep getting better at understanding everyone I taught and making sure they got what they needed from the experience. I was excited to meet new people and facilitators and grow alongside them. One of the things I valued most in leadership was the relational side, the work of genuinely understanding one another. That's why I was looking forward to working with both new and returning faces this semester.

What changed in your hopes and goals over the year?

At the start of this year, my primary goals were to improve my public speaking and refine my coaching skills. Serving as a facilitator gave me the perfect arena to practice speaking confidently and to coach a diverse group of students. Once I built that foundation, my focus shifted to a deeper question: how can I truly inspire others? During my semester as an intern, I leaned into my relational leadership style to genuinely connect with the incoming facilitators. My hope was no longer just to coach, but to motivate and inspire them to continuously grow and push their own limits as leaders.

How does what you have learned in this role connect to what you hope to do in the future?

Throughout my time in these roles, I have developed a diverse skill set that includes organizing groups, taking charge, public speaking, coaching, and empowering others to grow. In the future, I plan to join technical teams and ultimately step into leadership positions within the corporate world. My deep understanding of relational leadership will allow me to cultivate a positive, supportive work environment. I aspire to be a leader who delivers strong technical results while ensuring my teammates feel genuinely valued and heard. The experiences I have gained here perfectly position me to inspire and bring out the absolute best in every individual I work with.

02 · Metaphor

My internship has been… a metamorphosis.

When I started my college journey, I was firmly grounded. I hated the idea of being a leader because I associated it strictly with the "Great Man Theory," assuming you had to be the loudest, most extroverted person in the room. Enrolling in the Leadership minor was my catalyst for change. It dissolved those preconceptions, showing me that leadership comes in countless forms. That program broke me out of my shell and initiated my metamorphosis into a confident, self aware leader.

That transformation gave me the wings to become a co-president of Club Cricket and take the initiative in team settings. But if the minor taught me how to fly, this internship taught me how to lead the flock. It pushed me to a new level by allowing me to step into facilitator and intern roles, where I could share my passion for leadership studies. I was able to work in service of the students and peers around me, deeply aligning with my relational focus.

Coming into this internship, I was proud of my own ability to fly. Now, I am leaving with the tools and the drive to inspire everyone around me to take flight.

03 · Defining Moments

Moments that shaped the year.

  1. November, 2025

    Fall Facilitation

    In the Fall, I facilitated the Foundational Leadership track. I focused heavily on the practice of listening rather than telling, which I learned is the vital distinction between facilitating and simply teaching. It was fun to hear the perspectives of all the participants and see them open up throughout the sessions! It taught me the power of creating a space where people feel truly heard.

  2. November, 2025

    The Climbing Course!

    The Climbing Course was one of my highlights of the Fall facilitation! It was incredible to see the group come together and support each other as we tackled the course. I loved watching the support on the sidelines, especially for those afraid of heights. The challenge also was fun, and I made it to the top!

  3. February, 2026

    Leading Learning Communities

    Transitioning into the Learning and Development Intern role this semester, my primary responsibility was leading a team of facilitators through our Monday learning communities to tailor the program to their growth. The experience taught me how to put complex concepts into practice, becoming a central part of my own development by strengthening both my theoretical knowledge and my ability to collaborate effectively.

  4. April, 2026

    Thursday Track Supervision

    As part of my intern duties, I supervised the Thursday track alongside an amazing set of facilitators: Erica, Jessica, and Vivian. They were open, funny, and an absolute treat to supervise. I felt immense pride working with them, and watching them grow into their roles and develop as leaders was one of the most rewarding parts of the role.

05 · Future

What I would do differently.

If I could do this experience all over again, I would have loved to supervise the Tuesday and Wednesday tracks in addition to Thursday. While I was able to connect with each facilitator through individual meetings, my bond with the Thursday facilitators deepened significantly because I saw them in their element every week. My primary hope coming into this role was to connect and grow alongside new people, so taking on those extra tracks would have made my experience even more fruitful. Above all, it would have been amazing to see every facilitator put the lessons from our Monday Learning Communities into action.

What I am carrying with me.

As I step into future roles, I carry with me a deep understanding of how to truly connect with a team. This relational focus allows me to bring out the absolute best in my peers and collaborate effectively with diverse groups of people. My time here has also taught me how to balance professional boundaries with genuine personal connection, which I have learned is one of the most common challenges in leadership.

These experiences have made me fully confident in my ability to thrive in any team environment, and I am excited for what comes next!
